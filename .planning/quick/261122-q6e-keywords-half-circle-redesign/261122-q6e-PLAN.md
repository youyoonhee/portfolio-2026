# Quick 261122-q6e: shorter cover intro, and Keywords as a sage half-circle that switches on scroll

## User request (2026-11-22, in Korean, with decisions)
- **Layout reference:** a screenshot of a site hero.
  - A large centred two-line Korean heading sits at the top.
  - Below it, a small counter "(1/4)" and a thin vertical line drop into a very large half-circle, whose top arc is visible and whose bottom is cut off by the viewport.
  - Inside the circle there is one big English word in white, and "SCROLL DOWN" small near the bottom.
  - **Do not copy that site.** Use it as a layout idea only. No particle or video texture (the user said so explicitly).
- **The user wants:** the 2nd section (`#keywords`, "01 / Keywords") redesigned this way. The three keywords 반응형 웹 → 인터랙션 → AI 활용 move on ("슥슥 넘어감") as the user scrolls.
- **Decisions:**
  - **Circle colour:** sage green, with white text.
  - **Heading above the circle:** "화면 너머의 경험까지 섬세하게." This sentence moves out of the cover.
  - **Project links:** remove the three links under each keyword (`ul.keyword-list`). The circle shows only the keyword name.
- **Cover intro:** below the English h1, it becomes exactly "반응형 웹 퍼블리셔 유윤희 입니다." "앱, " is dropped too, as the user wrote it. Keep 1.125rem.

## Design
- **Section:** keep `section#keywords`, the eyebrow "01 / Keywords", and the three `h3.keyword-title` (반응형 웹 / 인터랙션 / AI 활용) as real text in the DOM, in this order.
  - "화면 너머의 경험까지 섬세하게." becomes the section's visible h2 (`#keywords-title`), no longer visually hidden.
  - Size it about like the reference: `--text-heading` or a bit larger, semibold, centred, `text-wrap: balance`.
- **Circle:**
  - A big decorative circle (div or pseudo-element, `aria-hidden`), roughly `min(140vw, 1500px)` in diameter and centred horizontally.
  - Its top arc starts below the counter, and its lower part is clipped by the stage.
- **Circle colour:** add a token. The plain sage `#a9b89b` fails white text, so pick a deeper sage close in hue to `--color-sage` / `--color-sage-strong`.
  - White on it must reach at least **4.5:1**, because the counter and SCROLL DOWN are small text.
  - Add the pair to `tools/check-contrast.mjs`, and keep the token as hex only in tokens.css.
- **Inside the circle:**
  - A thin 1px white vertical line from the circle top downward, above the keyword.
  - The current keyword (h3) is centred, white, big (about `clamp(32px, 5vw, 64px)`) and semibold.
  - "SCROLL DOWN" is small, white, uppercase, `lang="en"`, decorative, and near the bottom of the visible stage.
- **Counter:** "(1/3)", "(2/3)", "(3/3)" sits above the circle top in ink colour and updates with the active keyword. Mark it `aria-hidden`, because all three h3s are in the DOM anyway.

## Scroll switching
- **Reuse the current mechanism:** the sticky stage in `css/layout.css` (`.keywords[data-switch="on"]`, a tall track with a sticky 100svh stage) and `js/main.js` (scroll position drives `data-state` on `.keyword-block` and never intercepts scroll). Extend it rather than rewriting it.
- **Widths:** the switch now also runs on mobile and tablet, not only at 1024px and up; the user wants this interaction everywhere.
  - Keep the guards: `screen`, `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`, a sensible min-height (for example 500px), and `html.js`.
- **Transition:** the outgoing keyword slides up and fades, and the incoming one slides up from below and fades in. Use transform and opacity only, about 500ms, with the existing `--dur-switch` / `--ease` tokens. The counter changes with it.
- **Track length:** about 0.5–0.7 screen per keyword, as now.
- **Without the switch** (no JS, reduced motion, short viewport, print): show the heading, then the circle area with all three keywords stacked and visible in the same sage and white style.
  - No counter and no "SCROLL DOWN" in this state.
  - Nothing may stay hidden.
- **Removed links:** the focus-in handler for keyword links goes with the links. Remove the dead JS and CSS for `.keyword-list`.
- **Overflow:** no horizontal overflow from 320 to 1920px.
  - Keep the existing rule: no overflow on the sticky stage or its ancestors, because sticky breaks. Clip with `clip-path` or `overflow: clip` on the circle element itself, or rely on `html { overflow-x: clip }`.
  - Check that sticky still works.
- **Panel background:** the `.keywords::before` surface panel may no longer fit. Decide what reads best with the half-circle (likely the plain page background), and say so in the summary.
